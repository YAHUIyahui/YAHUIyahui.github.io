01 · Selected work

Products I built and shipped

Reframe

Agentic CBT tutor · project lead and sole developer · 2025–present

  • Multi-agent design: a deterministic planner walks a 16-concept curriculum graph; the LLM only phrases the teaching and returns structured mastery estimates.
  • Safety in code: risk screening runs before any model call; testing exposed and closed a gap in Cantonese suicidal-intent detection.
  • Production setup: five LLM providers behind one interface, row-level data isolation, 33 end-to-end automated tests, three languages.
  • Now the intervention arm of a three-arm randomized controlled trial.
