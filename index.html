01 · Research

Three lines of work

1

LLM agents that deliver psychological interventions

I build LLM-based systems that follow a therapy protocol, and I test whether they change symptoms. In Reframe, code decides what is taught and the model decides how it is said, so each session stays faithful to cognitive behavioural therapy.

Evidence: first-author RCT in JMIR mHealth and uHealth (effects on depression and loneliness strongest among students under financial stress); three-arm RCT of agentic AI, sequential generative AI and conventional digital CBT, submission planned December 2026.

2

Digital mental health for older adults

My doctoral research asks when digital interventions work for older adults, who adopts them, and how to adapt them to Chinese communities. Human guidance emerged as the main moderator of intervention effects.

Evidence: three-level meta-analysis of 110 RCTs on six healthy-ageing outcomes; survey of 663 adults aged 60+ across Hong Kong and the Mainland extending UTAUT2; RCT of a culturally adapted Cantonese mindfulness app with an active control and 83% retention at 12 weeks.

3

Evaluation and safety of mental health AI

Deployed systems need checks that hold up outside the lab. I calibrate LLM-as-judge ratings against counselling-trained human raters, and I test risk screening across languages. Testing Reframe exposed and closed a gap in detecting Cantonese expressions of suicidal intent.

Outputs: technical report on separating curriculum control from language generation; a 28-day course on evaluating psychology LLMs and agents, designed for workshop teaching.

02 · Systems & demos

Each system below was designed and developed by me. Live links open working versions.

Reframe Agentic CBT tutor

Live demoIn RCT

A six-module CBT programme for four user groups in Mandarin, Cantonese and English.

  • A deterministic planner walks a 16-concept curriculum graph with mastery criteria; the LLM returns structured mastery estimates that drive progression.
  • Every retained memory cites the user's own words; risk screening runs in code before any model call.
  • Runs on five LLM providers with row-level data isolation and 33 end-to-end automated tests.
2025–present · Project lead and sole developerOpen demo and 2-minute video →
